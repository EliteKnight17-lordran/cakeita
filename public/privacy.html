Cakeita privacy policy

Last updated November 9, 2026. Cakeita is a student prototype made for Build-Off 2026 (Manila).

The short version

You can order a cake without an account. If you sign in, we keep only what is needed to pass your cake request and quote between you and the baker. We never handle payments. You can delete everything yourself, any time.

Without an account

  • Your bakery, requests and quotes stay in your own browser on your own phone.
  • Requests and quotes travel as links that you send yourself, for example in Messenger. Our servers do not keep them.
  • Your location for the "bakers near me" map stays on your phone. Only map pictures are downloaded.

If you sign in with Facebook, Google or email

Sign-in is handled by Google Firebase Authentication. From Facebook we ask only for your public profile (name and photo) and email. We store, in Google Firebase (Cloud Firestore):

  • Your name, email and profile photo link, so you can see which account you are using.
  • For bakers: your bakery menu and costs (private to you), your public menu, and your map pin if you choose to list it. Pins are rounded to about 100 meters.
  • Cake requests sent to a baker (the customer's name, contact, cake details and inspo photo if added). Only that baker can read them.
  • Quotes and confirmations, readable only by someone who has the exact order link.
  • For customers: your list of cake requests, so it follows you to another phone.

We do not sell or share this data, and we do not use it for ads.

Photos and AI

Inspiration photos are shrunk on your phone first. If the baker uses a Cakeita account, the shrunk photo is stored with your request so only that baker can see it, and it is deleted when the baker deletes their account. Otherwise it is never saved. If smart reading is switched on, the request text or photo is sent once to Google Gemini to read, then discarded. Prices are always calculated by Cakeita's own code from the baker's menu.

Phone notifications

If you turn on alerts, your browser gives us an address that lets Cakeita send this phone a notification ("new cake request" for bakers, "your quote is ready" for customers). It contains no name or number. Turn alerts off any time in your browser's site settings.

How to delete your data

  1. Open cakeita.vercel.app → Account and sign in.
  2. Tap Delete my account, then Yes, delete everything.

This removes your sign-in and everything the account put in the cloud: bakery, public menu, map pin, inbox requests and photos, confirmations, quotes, phone alerts and your cake list. To clear the data kept on your phone, clear this site's data in your browser settings.

If you signed in with Facebook, you can also remove Cakeita from Facebook → Settings → Apps and websites.

Payments

Cakeita never asks for, shows or handles payments. Pay your baker directly the way you both agree.
