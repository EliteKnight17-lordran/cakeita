Cakeita terms of use

Last updated November 9, 2026. Cakeita is a student prototype made for Build-Off 2026 (Manila), offered free during the pilot.

What Cakeita is

Cakeita helps customers describe a custom cake and see designs that fit their budget, and helps home bakers price and approve quotes. Cakeita is a tool between you and the baker. It is not a bakery and it does not sell, bake or deliver cakes.

Prices and quotes

  • Prices shown before the baker approves are estimates, calculated from the baker's own menu and costs.
  • Only a quote the baker has approved is the baker's offer. The order is between the customer and the baker.
  • Sample bakeries and sample prices are fictional and are labeled "sample".
  • The 3D cake is a guide to the design, not a photo of the finished cake.

Payments

Cakeita never asks for, shows or handles payments. Pay your baker directly, the way you both agree. If anyone asks you to pay "through Cakeita", it is not us.

Bakers

  • You are responsible for your menu, your prices, food safety, and any permits your business needs.
  • Character and brand names that customers ask for are shown as generic themes. Use licensed toppers for real characters.
  • Only list your shop on the map if you really take orders. We may remove listings that are fake or abusive.

Fair use

Do not use Cakeita to send spam, harass anyone, or upload pictures you have no right to share. We may block accounts or remove data that breaks these rules.

The pilot

Cakeita is free while we test it. Features may change, and the service may be unavailable at times. We are not liable for lost orders or losses from using a prototype. If paid plans come later, we will say so clearly first and nothing will be charged without your choice.

Your data

See the privacy policy. You can delete your account and data yourself at any time.

Help and contact

Use Feedback and help in the app. A person on the Cakeita team reads every message.
