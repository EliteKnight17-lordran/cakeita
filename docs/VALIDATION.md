# Customer validation kit (talk to customers)

Goal: replace our hypotheses with real numbers before Nov 10. Takes about 2 hours across the team.

## A. Bakers (5–10 home bakers, 10 minutes each, Messenger or call)

Ask exactly these 3, then show the app:

1. "Last week, how many custom cake inquiries did you get, and how many became orders?" → H2
2. "For one custom quote, about how many messages and minutes does it take?" → H1
3. "When did you last change your prices? Do you know your cost for a 6-inch cake today?" → H3

Then: open the demo, paste one of their real (anonymized) inquiries in **Requests → paste a DM**. Ask: "Would you send this price? What would you change?"
